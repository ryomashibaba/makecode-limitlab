# B11-R5 D54 — Experience-volume first: design contract before any further mass modelling

**2026-10-29 JST. Design study only. Not a true-3D result, not G0, not art acceptance.**

## Why D54 is a different process
D50 became a warehouse when enclosure came first. D51 became three separate facilities when programs were given separate forms. D52 became a giant egg when one shell came first. D53 A/B returned to **massive wall-canyons** despite different mass shapes. All are PRE-G0 ART REJECT.

The key failure is *not* insufficient complexity, code, triangles, wall details or image difference. Both facade-first and program-label-first designs failed because they do not derive a singular dramatic spatial experience from the actual constraints of activity, inhabitation, light and movement.

## The D54 authoring order (do not reverse)
1. **Human experience spine:** one continuous walk with four readable experiences — arrival, near-maker observation, research encounter, upper inhabited overlook. Record eye positions, desired views, allowable proximity and full path height changes before making the mass.
2. **Required empty space:** reserve real maker equipment clearance, spectator separation, public encounter space, research work and natural light routes, and a private but visually connected lived-in room. Use actual three-dimensional void envelopes, not colored activity squares.
3. **Real path & safety assumptions:** create the whole traversable vertical route from a single numeric source for stair treads, landings, galleries and destination floor. Independently test representative body sweep and head clearance. Do not replace official 331/993 with local checks.
4. **Activity-dependent *inhabited* thickness:** construct occupied support, enclosing room thickness and structural transitions only where required by the functional envelopes. **No initial solid egg, parallel walls, giant roof, huge fins or slabs** from which to subtract a supposed city.
5. **Actual natural light & overlap:** model openings and canopy separately; ray intersection above a point is not daylight or indoor certification. Intersections may be tested, but their artistic and lived-in quality must be judged visually.
6. **Two-scale view composition:** one dramatic public interior with a human participant and a distinctive distant urban silhouette must come from *the same form*. If either image simply shows a wall canyon, warehouse, pavilion, egg, shelves or gallery, reject at the coarsest PRE-G0 stage.

## Negative controls and falsification checks
- Remove **maker** and recompute architecture: shared volumes, openings, public circulation, support and silhouette should be meaningfully affected, not merely remove equipment.
- Remove **research**, then **living**, independently: the change must be meaningful and explainable. Node counts and pixel change are not beauty metrics.
- Break upper landing alignment deliberately: independent readback must identify the now-disconnected route.
- Put a candidate load-bearing mass into an eye line and a test body sweep: independently flag the obstruction.
- Remove canopy in a designated weather-sheltered area: coverage check must fail; do not infer daylight from canopy hit counts.
- Rebuild under a second path and hash the GLB/PNG/JSON outputs before claiming local reproducibility. GitHub CI PASS requires actual remote CI execution.

## Stage gates
- **D54 PRE-3D:** evidence-backed spatial section + explicit void/line-of-sight/path envelopes. It may be rejected without any GLB.
- **D54 EARLY TRUE3D:** only if spatial section is promising, save and reload coarse 3D and inspect fixed overview, arrival, maker, research and living views.
- **D54 independent audit:** only after a candidate is not aesthetically trivially rejected. Test walking volume, room opening and sunlight indicators; keep technical LIMITED claims separate.
- **PRE-G0 ART:** explicit human-readable rejection or further study. Do not turn technical success into art acceptance.

## Critical alternative
A structurally well-connected but artistically bland gallery is **not** a good result. Conversely a cinematic void image with blocked stairs/absent real living rooms is not a real place. If activity-derived geometry continues reverting to conventional forms, halt repetitive procedural iterations and first rework the artist-authored spatial language and visual reference analysis; do not solve it by more parameters or decoration.
