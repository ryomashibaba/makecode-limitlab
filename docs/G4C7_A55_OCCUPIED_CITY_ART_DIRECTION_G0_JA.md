## Two distinct G0 research directions — neither selected

### Option A: CROSS-SECTION COMMONS / 交差する生活街区

- First compose **one shared occupied public crossroads**, not a pair of walls. Four identifiable destinations physically open into it: an active food market with deep shaded bays and counters, an occupied culture-making room with visible working zone, a quiet research/readership terrace with book-scale furnishing, and a residential threshold with balcony/window rhythm **above and behind** the shared destinations.
- The city section is shaped by **different occupied room depths and sectional light openings**, not by a continuous upper lid. Outside image must read the same system: city blocks of different occupied thickness, terraces connected visibly and skylit cuts leading into the civic heart. Multiple skyline masses may be present but cannot be detached pavilions.
- Main relapse risk: low campus, shopping mall, parallel linear shopfronts, repeating floor decks. Fail the G0 sketch if these describe its first glance.

### Option B: INTERLOCKED SKY NEIGHBORHOODS / 交差空街区

- Begin with **one vertical collective civic hall that is simultaneously open to outdoor daylight and linked to three distinct inhabited neighborhoods** at offset elevations: maker/studio frontage, public learning zone, and residential gardens. Public transport/vertical circulation appears as a clearly legible inhabited threshold, not a standalone tube suspended over void.
- Global silhouette should emerge from several thick **inhabited urban bodies with genuine multiple cross-connections**, including exposed edges with a human-scale window/terrace cadence. The public city is both outside and inside those same bodies. Resist making one giant shell, one dramatic diagonal brace, or an empty canyon the centerpiece.
- Main relapse risk: quarry/void, stair-step megastructure, bridge maquette, useless floating decks. Fail the G0 sketch if the scene still depends on roof or skeletal framing to become 'futuristic'.
